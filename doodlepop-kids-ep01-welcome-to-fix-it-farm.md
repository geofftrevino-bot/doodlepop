# DoodlePop Kids: Fix-It Farm
## Episode 1 — "Welcome to Fix-It Farm"

**Runtime target:** 8–10 minutes
**Audience:** Ages 2–6
**Theme:** Everyone has something useful to contribute, and big jobs are easier when we work together.

---

### CAST

| Character | Role | Voice Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **ROCCO** | Rooster. Runs the farm. | Warm, big, a little theatrical. A proud leader who means well. Think friendly ringmaster. |
| **CLOVER** | Rabbit. Tends the garden and crops. | Soft, patient, sing-songy. The calm one. |
| **SPROCKET** | Pig. Fixes everything. | Bright, busy, chatty. Thinks out loud while she works. |
| **NUGGET** | Chick. Sprocket's sidekick, helps everyone. | Tiny, breathy, enthusiastic. Short sentences. Lots of "I can help!" |

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:35)

**EXT. FIX-IT FARM — MORNING**

*Sunrise over a rolling patchwork of green and gold fields. The rainbow windmill turns slowly. The red barn sits quiet. A rooster silhouette rises on the fence post.*

**ROCCO**
Cock-a-doodle— *(big breath)* —**DOO!**

*Birds scatter. The windmill spins faster. A flower pops open.*

**ROCCO (CONT'D)**
Good morning, Fix-It Farm! Today is not just any day.

*He hops down, struts toward camera, and beams.*

**ROCCO (CONT'D)**
Today is our **first official day** on the farm!

*SMASH TO TITLE.*

---

## THEME SONG (0:35–1:05)

> *(Bouncy, banjo-and-handclaps, big group vocal)*
>
> **ALL:** Doodle-doodle-POP! It's Fix-It Farm!
> Sun's coming up over the big red barn!
> Rocco's got a plan and Clover's got seeds,
> Sprocket's got a wrench for whatever you need!
> **NUGGET:** And I've got… uh… this bucket!
> **ALL:** Doodle-doodle-POP! We're better together!
> Fix-It Farm, now come on, let's go!

---

## SCENE 1 — ARRIVAL (1:05–2:10)

**EXT. FARM GATE — CONTINUOUS**

*A little wagon rolls up, piled impossibly high with boxes, crates, watering cans, and one very wobbly toolbox. CLOVER pulls from the front. SPROCKET pushes from the back. NUGGET rides on top, holding a rake like a flag.*

**NUGGET**
Weeeeee're heeeeere!

**CLOVER**
*(setting down the handle, looking up)*
Oh… oh, it's **beautiful**.

**SPROCKET**
*(wiping her brow)*
It's got a workshop! Rocco, you didn't say it had a **workshop**!

**ROCCO**
*(gesturing grandly)*
It has everything! Fields, a barn, a windmill, and one gorgeous little tractor.

*He pats a small, rusty, sleepy-looking tractor by the fence. A bolt falls off. Everyone watches it roll away.*

**ROCCO (CONT'D)**
*(beat)*
…She just needs a moment.

**NUGGET**
*(picking up the bolt, holding it high)*
I got it! I got the moment!

---

## SCENE 2 — THE MESS (2:10–3:20)

**INT. BARN — CONTINUOUS**

*Rocco throws open the barn doors with a flourish. Dust billows. The four of them stare.*

*Inside: towers of boxes. Crooked shelves. A wheelbarrow upside down. Tangled hoses. A single sock hanging from a rafter for no reason.*

**ALL FOUR**
*(quietly)*
…Ohhhh.

**CLOVER**
*(pushing open a side door, revealing the garden)*
Rocco… the garden is all weeds.

**SPROCKET**
*(from the workshop, holding up two mismatched tool halves)*
Rocco… the tools are **everywhere**.

**NUGGET**
*(muffled, from inside a box)*
Rocco… I'm in a box.

**ROCCO**
*(straightening his hat, undaunted)*
Friends. This is not a mess.

*Beat.*

**ROCCO (CONT'D)**
This is a **to-do list**.

---

## SCENE 3 — EVERYONE'S JOB (3:20–5:00)

**MONTAGE — AROUND THE FARM**

*Rocco produces a giant scroll that unrolls to the floor and keeps going.*

**ROCCO**
I have made a Perfect Plan! Clover — garden. Sprocket — tractor. Nugget — you help Sprocket. And I will… *(flourish)* …**supervise**.

### SONG 2 — "EVERYBODY'S GOT A JOB"

> *(Marching, call-and-response, one verse per character)*
>
> **CLOVER:** Pull a weed, plant a seed, give the little sprouts a drink!
> **SPROCKET:** Turn a screw, tighten two, this old tractor's gonna zi-i-ing!
> **ROCCO:** Point and plan, that's my job, I'm the rooster with the chart!
> **NUGGET:** I don't know what mine is yet — but I'm helping with my heart!
> **ALL:** Everybody's got a job, a job, a job!

*Under the song:*

- **CLOVER** clears a row of weeds, revealing a hidden strawberry patch. She gasps with delight.
- **SPROCKET** has the tractor hood open, parts laid out in neat rows on a cloth.
- **ROCCO** points at things. Nothing happens. He points harder.
- **NUGGET** brings Sprocket a watering can instead of a wrench. Then a flowerpot. Then a boot. Sprocket gently sets each one aside without looking.

**SPROCKET**
*(hand out, eyes on the engine)*
Wrench, please.

**NUGGET**
*(slapping a carrot into her hand)*
Wrench!

**SPROCKET**
*(holds it up, squints, keeps working)*
…Close.

*Nugget scurries off, knocks a stack of flowerpots into a gentle domino line. They all land upright. Nugget freezes, then tiptoes away.*

---

## SCENE 4 — IT STOPS WORKING (5:00–6:15)

**EXT. FARM — LATE MORNING**

*The music sours slightly. Everyone is now stuck.*

**SPROCKET**
*(elbow-deep in the tractor)*
I need the spark plug. It's in a box. It's in **one** of nine hundred boxes.

**CLOVER**
*(straining against a huge planter barrel, ears flat)*
I just need— one— push—

**ROCCO**
*(alone, staring at his scroll, deflating)*
But the plan said… the plan said it would all be done by lunch.

*He looks up. Clover is stuck. Sprocket is stuck. Boxes everywhere.*

**ROCCO (CONT'D)**
*(small)*
Huh. My Perfect Plan gave everybody a job.
But it didn't give anybody a **partner**.

*Meanwhile — NUGGET, quietly tidying, drags a box across the barn. Something rattles inside. He stops. Tilts his head. Opens it.*

*Inside, nestled in straw: the spark plug. It gleams. A tiny "ding!" sound.*

**NUGGET**
*(whisper, eyes enormous)*
…Sprocket?

---

## SCENE 5 — THE HERO BEAT (6:15–7:15)

**EXT. TRACTOR — CONTINUOUS**

*Nugget waddles up, holding the spark plug over his head with both wings. It's almost as big as he is.*

**NUGGET**
Sprocket! Is this the thing?

*Sprocket turns. Her jaw drops.*

**SPROCKET**
Nugget. **That's the thing.** That is exactly the thing!

**NUGGET**
*(beaming)*
I found it while I was cleaning up my mess!

**ROCCO**
*(walking over, hat in wings)*
…Everybody. Come here a second.

*The four gather.*

**ROCCO (CONT'D)**
I thought a good plan meant everybody working **apart**. But Clover can't lift that barrel alone. Sprocket can't dig through a barn alone. And I can't do *any* of it just by pointing.

**CLOVER**
*(gently)*
So let's do it the other way.

**ROCCO**
*(grinning, hat back on)*
**Together.** New plan!

### SONG 3 — "BETTER TOGETHER"

> *(Building, joyful, everyone joins one at a time)*
>
> **ROCCO:** One little job is a great big hill—
> **CLOVER:** But four little hands make it easier still!
> **SPROCKET:** Hand me a tool and I'll hand you a smile—
> **NUGGET:** And I'll bring a bucket! …Or maybe a file!
> **ALL:** We're better, better, better together!
> At Fix-It Farm forever and ever!

*Under the song:*

- All four push the planter barrel. It slides easily. Clover cheers.
- Assembly line: Nugget passes boxes to Rocco, Rocco to Clover, Clover stacks them neatly on the shelves.
- Sprocket drops the spark plug in. Nugget hands her the wrench — **the actual wrench this time.** Sprocket gives him a tiny fist-bump with one hoof.
- Rocco holds the ladder while Clover hangs a flower basket.

---

## SCENE 6 — THE REVEAL (7:15–8:15)

**EXT. FARM — GOLDEN LATE AFTERNOON**

*Sprocket turns the key. The tractor coughs. Sputters. Coughs again.*

*Everyone leans in. Holds their breath.*

*The tractor ROARS to life — a cheerful, chugging, almost musical putt-putt-putt. Its headlights pop on like two happy eyes.*

**ALL FOUR**
**YAAAAAY!**

*SLOW PAN across the farm: the garden in tidy rows with flowers along the fence. The barn stacked neat, doors painted. Hoses coiled. The windmill spinning. The little tractor putt-putting proudly in the yard.*

*The four of them sit in a row on the fence, watching the sun go gold.*

**ROCCO**
You know what we did today?

**NUGGET**
Cleaned a farm!

**ROCCO**
*(putting a wing around him)*
We made it our **home**.

*Beat. Everyone leans into each other. Warm music swells.*

---

## SCENE 7 — BUTTON (8:15–8:45)

**EXT. BARN — CONTINUOUS**

**ROCCO**
*(standing, stretching, immensely satisfied)*
And the best part is… everything is **finally** exactly where it belongs.

**NUGGET**
Ooh! I'll put the bucket away!

*Nugget pulls open the barn door.*

*BEAT.*

*An avalanche of boxes tumbles out in a soft, bouncy, cartoon pile. A rubber chicken lands on Rocco's head. The single mystery sock drifts down and lands on Nugget.*

*Silence. Nugget peeks out from under the sock.*

**NUGGET (CONT'D)**
*(tiny)*
…I found more.

*Everyone bursts out laughing. Rocco laughs hardest of all, rubber chicken still on his head.*

**ROCCO**
*(through laughter)*
Tomorrow! We'll get it tomorrow!

*PULL BACK — the four of them laughing in a heap of boxes as the windmill turns and the sun sets.*

**ALL**
*(singing, ragged and happy)*
Doodle-doodle-POP! It's Fix-It Farm!

**END OF EPISODE**

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Pacing beats for the 8–10 min cut:**
Cold open → theme → arrival → the mess → everyone's job → breakdown → hero beat → teamwork song → reveal → button. Nine beats, roughly one per minute, which fits preschool attention spans.

**Nugget's running gag:** He hands Sprocket the wrong object three times (watering can, flowerpot, carrot). The payoff is the fourth time, when he hands her the *right* thing. Keep the count at three — kids this age anticipate the pattern by the third and the payoff lands.

**Safety/tone guardrails:** No one is ever scolded. Sprocket never gets frustrated with Nugget — she sets things aside kindly. The mess is never dangerous. Rocco's realization is his own; nobody tells him he was wrong.

**Repeatable series format established here:**
1. Rocco announces the day's job
2. Everyone splits into roles
3. Something goes wrong because they're working apart
4. Nugget stumbles into the solution
5. Teamwork song
6. Reveal + warm moment
7. Harmless chaos button

**Songs to produce:** 3 (theme ~30s, "Everybody's Got a Job" ~45s, "Better Together" ~45s). The theme is reusable across every episode.
