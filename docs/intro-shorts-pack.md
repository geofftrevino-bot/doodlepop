# DoodlePop Kids — Intro Shorts Pack
## 5 vertical Shorts · one generation each · publishable as-is

**Format:** 9:16 · 8–10 seconds · 1080p · single continuous shot
**Why this works:** one Runvid generation = one finished asset. No editing, no stitching, no consistency drift across cuts.

---

## THE RULES THAT MAKE THESE WORK

1. **Hook in the first 0.8 seconds.** Character already on screen, already moving. No fade-ins, no establishing shots, no logo first.
2. **One character, one trait, one gag.** A 9-second Short cannot hold two ideas.
3. **Face in the top third.** The bottom 30% of a vertical frame is buried under the title, handle, and description overlay.
4. **End on a loop-friendly beat.** A held pose or reset gesture makes the autoplay loop feel intentional rather than abrupt.
5. **Audio carries it.** Most Shorts autoplay with sound on. The crow, the engine, the "ding" — that's the hook as much as the visual.

## SHORT 1 — "WELCOME TO FIX-IT FARM" (the farm)
**Post first.** This is the channel's front door.

**Hook:** Rooster crow into a whip-pan reveal of the whole farm.
**Title:** Welcome to Fix-It Farm! 🌾
**Caption:** Meet the crew who lives here! #DoodlePopKids #KidsCartoon #FarmFriends

> [STYLE BLOCK] SHOT: Vertical 9:16. Open tight on ROCCO — a cheerful red-orange rooster wearing a tiny straw farmer's hat and a red kerchief — crowing joyfully straight at camera, filling the top half of the frame. Camera whip-pans right and pulls back to reveal the whole farm in one continuous move: a bright red barn, a rainbow-painted windmill turning, patchwork green-and-gold crop fields, a little tractor putting along, and three more characters waving up at camera from below — a soft white rabbit in a green gardening apron, a bright pink piglet in a brown tool belt, and a very small fluffy yellow chick in a blue cap. Golden morning light, confetti of flower petals in the air. Ends on a wide held shot of all four waving. Audio: a happy rooster crow, upbeat banjo and handclaps, cheerful group laughter.

**Title:** This is Rocco. He's got a PLAN. 📋
**Caption:** Rocco runs Fix-It Farm! #DoodlePopKids #KidsCartoon

## SHORT 3 — "THIS IS CLOVER"
**Trait:** Makes things grow. **Gag:** Everything blooms at once.

**Title:** This is Clover. Everything she touches GROWS. 🌻
**Caption:** Clover takes care of the whole garden! #DoodlePopKids #KidsCartoon

> [STYLE BLOCK] SHOT: Vertical 9:16. CLOVER — a soft white rabbit with long floppy ears wearing a green gardening apron with seed-packet pockets — kneels in a garden bed in the upper-middle of frame, gently patting soil with a tiny trowel. She scatters a handful of seeds and taps the ground twice. Instantly a whole row of flowers and vegetables springs up around her in a rippling wave — sunflowers, carrots, tomatoes, daisies — rising in sequence and filling the frame with color. Her ears perk straight up in delight and she gives a tiny happy clap. Audio: soft magical sparkle chimes rising in pitch, gentle ukulele, a delighted little gasp.

## SHORT 4 — "THIS IS SPROCKET"
**Trait:** Fixes anything. **Gag:** Instant transformation.

**Title:** This is Sprocket. She can fix ANYTHING. 🔧
**Caption:** Watch her bring this old tractor back to life! #DoodlePopKids #KidsCartoon

> [STYLE BLOCK] SHOT: Vertical 9:16. SPROCKET — a bright pink piglet wearing a brown tool belt and tiny welding goggles pushed up on her head — stands beside a small rusty broken-down tractor, centered in the upper two-thirds of frame. She pulls her goggles down over her eyes with a determined grin, spins a wrench in one hoof, and dives behind the tractor. A quick comic burst of sparks, puffs of smoke, and flying bolts. She pops back up, pushes the goggles onto her forehead, and slaps the hood. The tractor shudders, then roars to life — freshly painted bright red, headlights popping on like two happy eyes. She throws both hooves up in victory. Audio: clanking tools, a comic sputter, then a triumphant musical engine putt-putt-putt and a cheerful fanfare.

## SHORT 5 — "THIS IS NUGGET"
**Trait:** Helps with everything. **Gag:** Helps with the wrong thing.
**This one will outperform the others.** The runt who tries hardest is the most shareable character on any preschool channel.

**Title:** This is Nugget. He's… helping. 🐥
**Caption:** Nugget always wants to help! #DoodlePopKids #KidsCartoon #Funny

> [STYLE BLOCK] SHOT: Vertical 9:16. NUGGET — a very small fluffy yellow chick wearing a little blue cap — waddles eagerly into frame carrying a wrench far too big for him, held over his head with both wings. He proudly presents it up toward a bright pink piglet in a brown tool belt standing above him, who is holding out one hoof without looking. She takes it — and it's a carrot. She squints at the carrot, then down at him. NUGGET looks at the carrot, looks at camera, and gives a tiny innocent shrug and a hopeful grin, tail feathers wiggling. Face stays in the upper third of frame. Audio: tiny hurried footsteps, a comic woodblock beat, a soft confused musical "womp," and a small cheerful chirp.

## POSTING ORDER & CADENCE

| Order | Short | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome to Fix-It Farm | Establishes the world; pin this to the channel |
| 2 | Nugget | Strongest hook — lead with your most shareable character |
| 3 | Sprocket | Satisfying before/after transformation, high rewatch |
| 4 | Rocco | Anchors the leader before the first full episode |
| 5 | Clover | Calmest of the five; the gentle one lands better once the cast is known |

Space them 2–3 days apart. Each one should end up in the description of the others as a playlist.

## AFTER THESE FIVE

The repeatable Shorts formats this cast supports:

- **"Wrong Tool"** — Nugget hands over an absurd object. Infinitely repeatable, ~9 seconds each.
- **"Watch It Grow"** — Clover time-lapses a different crop each time. Naturally satisfying, very loopable.
- **"Fix It in 10 Seconds"** — Sprocket repairs something new. Before/after is the most reliable Shorts structure there is.
- **"Rocco's Plan"** — a plan that immediately goes wrong in a harmless way.
- **Song stings** — 10-second hooks from the episode songs. Music is what makes preschool content loop.
