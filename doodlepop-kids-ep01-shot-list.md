## THE STYLE BLOCK

Paste this at the front of **every** video prompt. Consistency comes from never varying it.

> Preschool 3D animated cartoon, felt/claymation style. Chunky rounded shapes, bold clean outlines, soft matte shading, big expressive glossy eyes, bright saturated primary colors, cheerful storybook lighting. No text, no logos, no on-screen words.

## PRODUCTION SEQUENCING

Don't shoot in script order. Shoot in this order:

1. **Shots 2, 3, 5** — finish the cold open while the style is fresh in the reference stills
2. **Shots 15, 22, 24, 75** — the big establishing plates. These define every set. Approve them as stills first, then animate.
3. **Shots 6–12** — the theme song, since it's reused across the entire series
4. **Everything else**, scene by scene

## COST CONTROL

- **Storyboard first.** Generate all 81 as stills with `generate_image` before animating anything. Stills are dramatically cheaper, and roughly a third of these shots will change once you see them in sequence.
- **Reuse plates.** Shots sharing a set (24, 29, 56, 69 are all `BARN_INT`) should animate from the same approved still.
- **Hold the long shots for last.** Shots 75 and 81 are the longest and most expensive — generate them once the style is fully locked.
- **Budget for 1.5× overage.** Plan on roughly 120 generations for 81 finished shots.
